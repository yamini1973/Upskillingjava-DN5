Community Bulletin

The Lavender Bloom Festival celebrates local creativity through music, art and educational workshops. Visitors can attend live concerts, participate in baking sessions, join painting exhibitions and support local businesses throughout the season. Families can enjoy outdoor activities, interactive games and cultural programs organized by community volunteers. The event portal demonstrates JavaScript, DOM manipulation, forms, filtering, asynchronous programming, event handling and modern ES6 concepts.
